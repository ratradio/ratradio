Identity

RAT Radio is an independent broadcasting platform and digital archive dedicated to the uncompromised exploration of sound. We operate as a fluid space for musical exploration, connecting the dots between obscure sub-genres and global movements.

Curation

We prioritize human curation over algorithms. Every broadcast is a documented session, focusing on the preservation and transmission of sounds that exist outside the commercial spectrum.

Presence

We do not seek the spotlight, we are the shadow that defines it. Moving beneath the surface of the mainstream, we are the foundation for the uncompromising, the obscure, and the essential.
